Problem and solution

Research planning is difficult before the research has even started.

Early ideas are often too broad, methods are selected without clear evidence needs, and risks appear only after implementation. The assistant makes those decisions visible and reviewable from the beginning.

The problem

A topic is not yet a research design

Students must translate interest into a realistic scope, answerable questions, suitable methods, measurable criteria and a deliverable plan.

  • Broad or ambiguous questions
  • Weak links between method and evidence
  • Unexamined feasibility and project risk
The solution

One structured planning conversation

A local scaffold establishes the complete schema. SAIA develops the final content, and Pydantic accepts it only when the technical structure is valid.

  • Transparent research-question checks
  • Connected methodology and evaluation guidance
  • Reusable history and exportable outcomes
Target users

Designed for students who need a credible starting point.

Master’s students Capstone teams Early-stage researchers Academic supervisors

Important limitations

A structured plan still requires human academic judgment.

01

Final generation requires a valid Academic Cloud key and an available SAIA service.

02

Schema validation cannot establish scientific correctness or factual accuracy.

03

LLM recommendations can be incomplete, biased or unsuitable for a specific course.

04

Local history is a single-user prototype, not an authenticated collaborative store.
